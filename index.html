What is it

What it means to stake WBTC

WBTC is an ERC-20 token that represents Bitcoin on Ethereum. Bitcoin has no staking, and neither does the wrapper, so staking WBTC is a loose term. In practice it means depositing WBTC into a protocol that pays you for providing it.

Where the reward comes from

  • Lending markets: you supply WBTC and borrowers pay interest to use it.
  • Liquidity pools: you add WBTC beside another asset and earn a share of trading fees, with impermanent loss as a risk.
  • Restaking and vault protocols: some accept WBTC and pay extra rewards for it, in exchange for extra risk.

What does not change

Your WBTC is still a token backed by BTC held by a custodian, and it is still exposed to the price of Bitcoin. Depositing it adds smart contract risk on top. The reward is payment for taking that risk, not a guaranteed return.

Lend vs restake

Lending WBTC vs restaking vault

Both put WBTC to work, but they pay for different things and fail in different ways. Check which one you are depositing into before you sign.

In practice

Lending is the simpler of the two and has fewer moving parts. A restaking vault can pay more because you accept more risk and often less flexibility. Read the withdrawal terms first.

How to stake

How to stake WBTC

Pick the destination

Decide whether you want lending, a pool or a vault. The interface shows the terms and the contract you are about to use, and nothing moves until you sign.

Step by step

  1. Connect your wallet and make sure it holds WBTC and a little ETH for gas.
  2. Choose how to stake and enter an amount. WBTC uses 8 decimals, so amounts can go down to eight places.
  3. Approve WBTC once for the protocol contract, since it is an ERC-20 token.
  4. Sign the deposit and wait for confirmation on Ethereum.
  5. To exit, sign a withdrawal. Some protocols pay out at once, others queue it.

If you only want to hold Bitcoin exposure and not earn on it, you do not need any of this.

Fees & gas

What staking WBTC costs

Gas

Each on-chain step is a separate Ethereum transaction paid in native ETH: the approval, the deposit and later the withdrawal. See how gas works. WBTC does not pay for its own gas, so keep ETH in the wallet.

Protocol charges

Depending on the destination, there may be a performance fee, a withdrawal fee or a share of rewards taken by a vault. Pools also cost you through impermanent loss when prices move apart. These show on the confirmation screen or in the protocol terms.

Small deposits

Because gas does not scale with the amount, a small deposit can lose a large part of its reward to fees. Compare the gas estimate to what you expect to earn.

Security

Staying safe

Non-custodial by design

You connect your own wallet and sign every transaction yourself. This interface only prepares the transaction. It never holds your WBTC or your keys.

Before you sign

  • Verify the WBTC contract address, since fake wrapped Bitcoin tokens exist.
  • Approve only the amount you plan to deposit, and revoke old ERC-20 allowances you no longer use.
  • Understand the withdrawal terms and any lock-up before depositing.
  • Remember that WBTC depends on its custodian and each protocol adds its own contract risk, so the risk is never zero.
FAQ

Stake WBTC FAQ

Can you really stake WBTC?

Not in the strict sense, because WBTC is an ERC-20 wrapper of BTC and has no native staking. When people say they stake WBTC, they mean depositing it into a lending market, a liquidity pool or a vault that pays for it. The reward and the risk depend on which one you use.

Where does the yield on staked WBTC come from?

It depends on the destination. Lending pays interest from borrowers, pools pay a share of trading fees, and vaults or restaking protocols pay rewards set by the protocol. None of these is guaranteed and each carries its own risk.

Why do I need to approve WBTC first?

WBTC is an ERC-20 token, so a protocol contract needs an allowance before it can move your tokens. The approval is its own transaction and costs gas. You can limit it to the amount you plan to deposit.

Do I need ETH to stake WBTC?

Yes. Gas on Ethereum is paid in native ETH, not in WBTC. Keep enough ETH for the approval, the deposit and the later withdrawal.

What are the risks of staking WBTC?

The main ones are smart contract bugs, impermanent loss in pools, liquidation and market events in lending, and extra layers of risk in restaking vaults. WBTC also relies on its custodian, and its price follows Bitcoin. Only deposit what you can afford to have locked or lose.

How many decimals does WBTC have?

WBTC uses 8 decimals, the same as Bitcoin, unlike most ERC-20 tokens which use 18. The smallest amount you can enter is 0.00000001 WBTC. Mind the decimals if you copy raw amounts between tools.
